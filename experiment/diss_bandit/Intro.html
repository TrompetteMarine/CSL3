In this study you will trade in a simple ticket market for 450 rounds. Each round you receive one free ticket of your choice — SQUARE or TRIANGLE. After you choose, the market settles: your ticket either pays 1 point or pays nothing.

How settlement works, exactly: settlements are generated by an automated market maker from a payout rule that was fixed before your session, using a pre-drawn random sequence. The displayed market prices are the market maker's standing quotes and are held fixed; no other trader moves them during your session. How often a ticket pays can change during the study without warning, as set by the rule.

The payout rule used in your session was set for your group as a whole at a fixed rate.

{{ else }}

The payout rule used in your session is one of several rules used across sessions of this study.

{{ endif }}

In addition, every 15th round you receive an announced windfall of {{ C.BONUS_POINTS }} points, regardless of what you chose. Tickets are house-funded: you never pay for a ticket and you cannot lose points on a trade.

Throughout the task a trader board shows what another participant who previously completed this same task won on each of their rounds, and their overall rate. This is a real record from a previous session, selected from prior participants of this study — not a simulation.

Every few rounds we will ask you two quick questions: whether the market settles fairly, and what you feel you deserve to win per round. Occasionally we will also ask for a quick forecast about the next settlement — a more accurate forecast gives you a higher chance of a bonus — and, at two fixed points, you will get a one-time option to pay from a separate 20-point allowance to have the payout rule replaced with a fresh rule generated the same way (unspent allowance is paid out).

In this study you will make 450 choices between two boxes. Each choice can earn you 1 point. Some boxes pay more often than others, and how often a box pays can change during the study without warning.

In addition, every 15th choice you receive an announced bonus of {{ C.BONUS_POINTS }} points, regardless of what you chose.

Throughout the task you will see what another participant who previously played this same task earned on each of their choices. This is a real record from a previous session, not a simulation.

Every few choices we will ask you two quick questions: how fair you find the payout rule, and what you feel you deserve to earn per choice.

The payout rule used in your session is one of several rules used across sessions of this study.

Your points are converted to money at the rate shown at the end. The study takes about 28 minutes. You may stop at any time, but payment requires completion.
