# Technique decisions — what each method does for this pipeline (I032)

The decided table, not a reading list. One card per technique: the
identification problem it solves, the bias it removes, its cost, and the
decision with its reason. Every citation is verified at the level stated in
`freezes/00_citation_verification_I032.md` (V-claim = the relied-on claim was
read; V-biblio = positioning only). A rejection is as binding as an adoption.

| # | Technique | Problem solved / bias removed | Cost | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Induced institution: seeded settlement, phase rates fixed by design (Smith 1976, V-biblio) | Makes "the game is rigged" objectively false, which disciplines every rival | none | **ADOPT** (in place) | The core of the design |
| 2 | LMSR-style moving market maker (Hanson 2007, V-biblio) | Incentive-compatible price signal | moving prices | **REJECT** as a mechanism; keep a truthful fixed-quote market maker | Moving prices would leak the invisible phase boundaries and open a second channel ("the price moves against me") for rigging beliefs. Fixed quotes are disclosed as fixed, so the description stays true |
| 3 | Minimal-information binarized scoring rule (Hossain–Okui 2013; Danz–Vespa–Wilson 2022, V-claim) | Settlement-probability reports robust to risk attitude; centre-pull from incentive detail avoided | ~30 reports, ~1 min | **ADAPT**: exploratory only, details on request, legitimacy never scored | Charness–Gneezy–Rasocha (2021, V-claim) find introspection about as good as complex incentivized methods; the report layer earns no confirmatory role |
| 4 | Becker–DeGroot–Marschak for the rule-switch WTP (1964, V-claim) | Truthful maximum price (weak dominance) | 2 probes | **ADOPT, wording sharpened** | With "fresh rule generated the same way", a participant can believe switching redraws the *rates*, so WTP mixes distrust with a rate lottery. The re-seed redraws only the random sequence; the screen now says so. Fair-belief WTP is then exactly 0, and positive WTP reveals the "draws arranged against me" belief. House-money inflation of WTP levels (Thaler–Johnson) is common to both arms, so the contrast is unaffected |
| 5 | Demand treatments to bound experimenter demand (de Quidt–Haushofer–Roth 2018, V-claim) | Bounds demand-driven responses | ≥2 extra arms | **REJECT the arms; ADOPT the bound as an anchor** | Instructions are identical across HIGH/LOW, so instruction-borne demand differences out of the confirmatory contrast. Arm-specific demand requires inferring the hypothesis, which the funnel measures. DQHR's bounds under *explicit* demand average ~0.13 SD across 11 tasks; implicit demand here is plausibly smaller. Sensitivity: H1a re-run excluding participants whose blind-coded purpose guess names the reference hypothesis |
| 6 | Non-deceptive obfuscation (Zizzo 2010, V-claim) | Demand matters when **positively correlated with the true hypothesis**; obfuscation breaks the correlation | none | **ADOPT** (in place) | Invisible phases, several co-measures, and the "one of several rules" line obscure the focal fairness-by-peer contrast without deception |
| 7 | Instructional manipulation checks (Oppenheimer et al. 2009, V-claim) | Satisficing adds variance and masks effects | ~1 min | **ADAPT: comprehension quiz, repeat until correct, no exclusion** | The authors prefer prompting rereading to excluding failers, since exclusion can bias the sample. The quiz covers the market rules and the peer's provenance, which also guards believability. No new exclusion is created |
| 8 | Prolific panel; approval filter (Peer et al. 2022, V-claim) | Platform data quality | n/a | **ADOPT Prolific; re-describe the approval filter** | Prolific was the only source with high quality on all four aspects without filters. Approval rating did *not* predict quality, so ≥97% is kept as a standard filter but no longer cited as quality control |
| 9 | Studentized permutation Welch (Chung–Romano 2013, V-claim) | Level validity under unequal variances; the unstudentized test can err in level and even in sign | compute only | **ADOPT** (co-report, in place) | Exactly the heteroskedastic two-arm setting of H1a |
| 10 | Randomized slider starts (ISR slider-defaults study, V-claim; authors pending) | On 101-point sliders a fixed default, midpoint included, draws excess responses at that value | none | **ADOPT + two consequences** | (a) Randomized starts turn default anchoring into noise orthogonal to condition, and the start value is logged. (b) They **defeat the frozen zero-variance screen**: a never-mover now reports a fresh random value each time. The screen is translated to *zero movement* (reported = logged start on every rating), which restores its intent with no new researcher freedom. (c) Slider step set to 5 (21 points), matching the round-to-5 measurement model the certification passed under |
| 11 | Dual learning rates α⁺/α⁻ (Lefebvre et al. 2017; Palminteri–Lebreton 2022, V-claim) | Asymmetric updating | fit only | **ADAPT: exploratory, difference-based only** | Positivity asymmetry is general across contexts and species, so a level asymmetry is not diagnostic of the gate. Only the HIGH-vs-LOW difference can carry information, which matches the I015 record (level sanity check failed) |
| 12 | Self-other paradigm in dmPFC (Wittmann et al. 2016, V-claim) | Neural tracking of others' performance | fMRI | **REJECT the paradigm; keep the region as a downstream target** | It used confederates and predetermined feedback, which is deception and incompatible with our truthful-display protocol. Its finding (dorsomedial area 9 tracks the other's performance) names where an imaging arm would look for the trader-board signal |
| 13 | vmPFC lesion prosocial learning (Lockwood et al. 2024; Gueguen et al. 2025, V-claim for findings) | Causal dissociability of socially contextualized valuation | lesion cohort | **ADOPT as a downstream target only** | Event-timing details not verified, so no timing constraint is imported from them |
| 14 | Jittered inter-trial intervals (Dale 1999, V-claim) | Event-related BOLD efficiency, >10× over fixed ISIs | session length | **ADAPT: config knob, off online** | Shared-engine requirement for the companion EEG/imaging study. Online keeps the 350 ms fixed gap to hold the ~28-min session. Configured as `iti_jitter_ms` (e.g. [500, 2500] per Wittmann's range) |
| 15 | Strong inference; severity (Platt 1964; Mayo 2018, V-biblio) | Inference-tree logic | none | **Positioning only** | Already the logic of TASK_INFERENCE.md |
| 16 | Motivated reasoning; belief production (Kunda 1990; Bénabou–Tirole 2016, V-biblio) | Distinguishes belief distortion from a gate on evidential authority | none | **Positioning only** | The task separates the two (TASK_INFERENCE.md §4) |
| 17 | Partisan judgment bias in prediction markets (Forsythe et al. 1992, V-claim) | Evidence that motivated distortion survives market discipline | none | **Positioning (verified)** | 62%/68% of supporters net-bought their own candidate (§III Table 8); the bias sits among non-marginal traders (§IV) |

## Build changes this table forces (implemented in B, I032)

1. WTP screen wording: "the chances that each ticket pays stay exactly as set; only the random draws are replaced" (#4).
2. Comprehension quiz with repeat-until-correct; no exclusion attached (#7).
3. Slider `step=5` on fairness, entitlement and forecast sliders (#10c).
4. Zero-movement screen in `pipeline.py` replacing zero-variance under randomized starts; a pilot-calibrated partial-mover threshold is reserved for the pilot (#10b).
5. `iti_jitter_ms` session-config knob, default off (#14).
