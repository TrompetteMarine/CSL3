# Citation verification log — KGi-DISS-I032

Rule (carried from I004): no citation enters a deliverable unless verified
against a primary text or an authoritative bibliographic record, and the
specific claim it carries is checked, not just its existence. Levels used here:

- **V-claim** — the claim the design relies on was read in the primary text or
  its abstract (location given).
- **V-biblio** — bibliographic record confirmed (venue, volume, pages); the
  claim is a standard positioning statement not re-read this iteration. May be
  cited for positioning only, never as the basis for a design decision.
- **PENDING** — not verified; may not be cited.

Fetched 2026-11-09. Sources listed as visited.

## Discrepancies found and how they are handled

1. TASK_INFERENCE.md §1 attributes "contagion of wishful thinking" to no source
   (it sits next to the IEM citation). Source is Seybert & Bloomfield 2009. Fix
   in E: attribution added. The IEM partisan-trader claim itself is verified.
2. DESIGN.md §2 uses "approval ≥ 97%" as if it were a quality filter. Peer et
   al. find reputation does not predict quality. Fix in E: the filter is kept
   (harmless, standard) but re-described; data quality is carried by the
   in-task screens, not by approval rating.
